☀ Solar Energy

A simple way to make energy from sunlight

What is solar energy?

Solar energy comes from the Sun. Solar panels collect sunlight and turn it into electricity that we can use in homes, schools, and other buildings.

Why is it useful?

  • Sunlight is a renewable source of energy.
  • Solar power can help reduce air pollution.
  • It can reduce our use of fossil fuels.

Solar energy in Egypt

Egypt has abundant sunlight during the year. This makes solar energy a useful option for producing electricity. Using more solar power could help Egypt depend less on fossil fuels.

What can we do?

We can learn about renewable energy, share information with friends, and look for ways to save electricity in our daily lives.
